The Silk Road played an important role in the development of trade and cultural connections between East and West. Along this route, silk, spices, and metal goods were transported from China to Europe, while religious teachings and scientific knowledge also spread. Cities along the route became important trading centres where merchant caravans would stop to rest. Regional rulers created special forces to protect trade routes and built caravanserais for travellers. Thanks to the flourishing trade, local crafts and architecture also developed. Historians consider this period one of the most active trade and economic stages in the region's history. Archaeological findings help scholars map ancient trade routes more precisely today.
