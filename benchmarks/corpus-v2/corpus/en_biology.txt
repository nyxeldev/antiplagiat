During photosynthesis, plants convert solar energy into chemical energy. This process takes place in the chloroplasts of leaves with the help of the pigment chlorophyll. Carbon dioxide and water combine to produce glucose and oxygen. The resulting glucose serves as the plant's main energy source, while oxygen is released into the atmosphere. The rate of photosynthesis depends on light intensity, temperature, and carbon dioxide concentration. Scientists are researching ways to improve photosynthetic efficiency in order to increase agricultural crop yields. This process is considered the starting point of the energy chain for nearly all living organisms on Earth.
