In modern pedagogy, active learning methods aim to develop students' ability to think independently. Unlike traditional lecture-based classes, active methods place the student at the centre of the process and devote more time to practical activities. Group discussions, project work, and the analysis of problem situations are the main tools of this approach. Research shows that active participation has a positive effect on the long-term retention of knowledge. At the same time, teachers require additional preparation to apply these new methods. Educational institutions are also using modern technologies when introducing these approaches.
