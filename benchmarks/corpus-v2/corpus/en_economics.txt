Inflation refers to a sustained rise in the general level of prices over time. Central banks use changes in interest rates as a tool to control inflation. High inflation reduces the purchasing power of the population and increases economic uncertainty. At the same time, extremely low inflation or deflation can also be dangerous for an economy, since it slows down investment and consumption. Economists regard a stable and predictable inflation rate as a favourable condition for economic growth. Fiscal policy and monetary policy together are the main factors influencing the inflation rate.
