In classical Eastern poetry, the ghazal genre occupies a special place. A ghazal usually portrays love, the philosophy of life, and human experience, is written in couplet form, and strictly follows a rhyme scheme. In the work of Alisher Navoi, this genre was developed with exceptional artistic skill, expressing deep philosophical meanings through symbolic imagery. Poets of later periods continued the classical traditions while bringing their own style and content. Literary scholars analyse the structure and content of the ghazal using traditional methods of interpretation. This genre continues to find expression in the work of contemporary poets today.
