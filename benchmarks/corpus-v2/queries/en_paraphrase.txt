Plants transform energy from sunlight into chemical energy through the process known as photosynthesis. This reaction occurs inside leaf chloroplasts, where the pigment chlorophyll plays a key role. Water and carbon dioxide are combined to generate oxygen and glucose. The glucose produced becomes the plant's primary energy supply, while oxygen is released back into the air. Light intensity, temperature and the concentration of carbon dioxide all influence how quickly photosynthesis proceeds. Researchers are studying ways to boost the efficiency of this process in order to raise crop yields in agriculture. Nearly every living organism on the planet depends, in some way, on this process as the starting point of its energy chain.
