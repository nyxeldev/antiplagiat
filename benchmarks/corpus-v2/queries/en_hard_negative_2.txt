Archaeological excavations in the Fergana Valley have revealed layers of occupation dating back to the Bronze Age, indicating continuous human settlement in this fertile region for millennia. The material culture of these early inhabitants shows connections with both steppe and agricultural civilisations. Burial mounds, ceramics, and bronze tools recovered from sites in the valley help reconstruct the lifeways of communities that preceded the historically documented Fergana polity. Recent isotopic analyses of skeletal remains shed light on ancient diet, mobility patterns, and trade contacts.
