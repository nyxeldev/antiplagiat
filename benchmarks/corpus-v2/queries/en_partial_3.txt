Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. While climate has always varied naturally, scientific evidence shows that human activities — primarily the burning of fossil fuels and deforestation — have been the dominant driver of climate change since the mid-twentieth century. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) has concluded with high confidence that global average surface temperatures have already risen by approximately 1.1 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.
