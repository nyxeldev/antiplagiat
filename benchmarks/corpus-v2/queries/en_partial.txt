In classical Eastern poetry, the ghazal genre occupies a special place. A ghazal usually portrays love, the philosophy of life, and human experience, is written in couplet form, and strictly follows a rhyme scheme. In the work of Alisher Navoi, this genre was developed with exceptional artistic skill, expressing deep philosophical meanings through symbolic imagery.
