Every morning before work, Daniyar takes his dog for a short walk around the block near his apartment building. The dog, a small terrier named Max, enjoys sniffing along the hedges and occasionally stopping to greet other dogs out for their own morning walks. On weekends, the walk is usually longer, sometimes extending all the way to the small park a few streets away, where Max can run freely in the fenced area. Daniyar has noticed that these short walks have become a pleasant routine that helps him start the day calmly before the usual rush of commuting and emails begins. His neighbours often joke that Max probably knows the neighbourhood better than any of the residents do.
