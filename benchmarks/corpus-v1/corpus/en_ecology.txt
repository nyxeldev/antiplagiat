The drying up of the Aral Sea is one of the most severe ecological problems in the region. Excessive use of river water for irrigation led to a sharp reduction in the sea's volume. This change altered the local climate, increased soil salinity, and negatively affected the health of the local population. Salt and dust storms rising from the exposed seabed are reducing agricultural yields. International organisations and countries in the region are cooperating to improve water resource management in order to mitigate the problem. Ecologists note that fully restoring the situation is difficult, but the consequences can be reduced.
